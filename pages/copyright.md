<h1 class="mb-3">Copyright Notice</h1>

Periodicals and books published in the United States before 1 January 1923 are in the public domain. If re-publishing, quoting from, or reproducing images from this digital archive, we request that you cite as follows: Lone Woman and Last Indians Digital Archive, [https://www.nps.gov/subjects/islandofthebluedolphins/index.htm](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/islandofthebluedolphins/index.htm).

Periodicals and books published in the United States on or after 1 January 1923 may be protected by US Copyright Law. All attempts to secure permission for republishing post-1923 material in this archive have been made. The names, logos, and identifying images, as well as the content of the articles, remain the property of the periodicals and/or the authors and artists themselves. Further reproduction, as well as quotation beyond fair use, is prohibited; permission must be obtained from the copyright holder.

Archival material published in this archive is used with permission of the institution or library that holds it. Further reproduction, as well as quotation beyond fair use, is prohibited; permission must be obtained from the copyright holder.

Responsibility for any use of the contents of the Lone Woman and Last Indians Digital Archive rests exclusively with the user.

If re-publishing, quoting from, or reproducing images from this digital archive with original publication in Australia, we request that you cite the original source provided via Trove, as well as the Lone Woman and Last Indians Digital Archive (Lone Woman and Last Indians Digital Archive, [https://www.nps.gov/subjects/islandofthebluedolphins/index.htm](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/islandofthebluedolphins/index.htm)).
